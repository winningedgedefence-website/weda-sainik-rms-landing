WHY CHOOSE WEDA?

// Preparation built on structure, mentorship and discipline

Led by experienced faculty and defence veterans since 2019, WEDA provides structured academic preparation, complete mentorship and personality development for students aspiring to join prestigious military schools.

Experienced Faculty

Educators who understand the requirements, examination patterns and preparation strategies for military school entrance examinations.

Veteran-Led Leadership

Guidance and strategic direction from defence veterans who bring real military values, discipline and leadership into the learning environment.

Complete Mentorship

Mentorship covering academic progress, doubt resolution, study planning, discipline and individual development.

Multiple Exam Preparation

Sainik School, Rashtriya Military Schools, UP Sainik School (UPSS) and Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya (JNV).

Physical Fitness & Personality Development

Physical fitness, confidence, communication, discipline and overall personality development.

Weekly assessments to evaluate performance, identify learning gaps and improve examination readiness.
