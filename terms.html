3. The demo classes

We offer 5+1 days of demo classes so a family can see our teaching before committing to a full course. A counsellor tells you the registration fee, if any, when they call you back; it is never collected on this page.

  • What it buys. Attendance in the live batch for those days, with the same faculty, the same classes and the same material as an enrolled student.

We are a defence-preparation academy and we hold students to the standard the services would. We expect punctuality, civility to faculty and fellow students, honesty in tests, and care of the premises.

We may ask a student to leave the course, without a refund, for:

  • Cheating in an internal test or mock examination.

  • Possession or use of alcohol, tobacco or any prohibited substance on our premises.
  • Wilful damage to the premises, equipment or property of others.
  • Sharing, recording, reselling or republishing our study material or classes — see clause 8.

Except where the conduct is serious enough to act on at once, we will speak to the parent before we take a student off a course.

We promise to teach. Qualified faculty, the hours you paid for, honest assessment of where your child stands, and a straight answer when you ask how they are doing.

We do not promise selection. No coaching institute can, and any institute that tells you otherwise is not being honest with you. Admission to a Sainik School, Rashtriya Military School, RIMC, the NDA or any other institution is decided by that institution and its selecting authority, on its own criteria — written marks, medical fitness, document verification, availability of seats in a category, and in some cases an interview. None of that is in our hands.

Any results we publish are the genuine achievements of past students. They are a record, not a forecast. A student's outcome depends above all on their own work.
