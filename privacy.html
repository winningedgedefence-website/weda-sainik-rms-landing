PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated 21 November 2026 · The Winning Edge Defence Academy, Dehradun

Most of the information we hold belongs to children. We have written this page to say plainly what we collect, why, and what you can ask us to do about it. It is the same policy that governs our main site, thewinningedge.co.in.

Who we are

The Winning Edge Defence Academy (“WEDA”, “we”, “us”) is a defence-entrance coaching institute operating since 2019 from WEDA Tower, Kailash Vihar, Sunderwala, Dehradun 248008, Uttarakhand, India.

We are the data fiduciary for the personal information described on this page. If you want anything here explained, corrected or acted upon, our contact details are at the bottom.

The short version

  • We collect what we need to teach your child, and not much else — contact details, admission details, fee records, attendance and test performance.
  • We do not sell or rent your information. Not to publishers, not to other institutes, not to anyone.
  • This page sets no tracking cookies and runs no analytics. We do not know who visits it.
  • Most of our records are about minors. We treat them as the parent's or guardian's to control, and we act on a parent's instruction.
  • You can ask us to show, correct or delete what we hold. Write to us and we will respond within thirty days.

What we collect

When you enquire from this page. Every button here — Enquire, Apply for Admission and Register Now — opens our enquiry form at crm.thewinningedge.co.in. It collects the name, phone number, email address, date of birth and area of interest you type in. We also record the date and time of the enquiry and that it came from this page, so our counselling team knows what you were reading. That is the whole of it — there are no hidden fields.

When you call or message us. We keep a record of calls and WhatsApp conversations with our counselling team — the number you contacted us from, what was discussed and what was agreed. This is how we avoid asking you the same questions twice and how we honour what a counsellor promised you.

When a student is admitted. At admission we collect, from the parent or guardian:

  • The student's details — full name, date of birth, gender, present school and class, target examination.
  • The parent or guardian's details — name, relationship, phone number, email address, residential address.
  • Documents you choose to give us — a photograph, proof of date of birth, school records, and where a reserved category or defence quota is claimed, the relevant certificate.
  • Fee records — amounts due, amounts received, dates, and the mode of payment.

We do not store card numbers, UPI PINs, net-banking passwords or CVV codes. No payment is taken on this page at all. Where a payment is made digitally, it passes through the bank or payment provider and we see only the confirmation.

While the course runs. We record attendance, test and mock-examination scores, and teaching notes — a faculty member's observations on where a student is struggling and what was tried. Parents are entitled to see all of it, and we would rather you asked.

Photographs and video at the academy. We photograph and film classes, camps, prize ceremonies and physical training, and we publish some of it on our website and on our YouTube, Instagram and Facebook pages. Students appear in these images. If you do not want your child photographed or published, tell us in writing and we will respect it — before admission or at any time afterwards. There is no penalty for saying no, and it changes nothing about the teaching your child receives. If a photograph of your child is already published and you want it taken down, write to us and we will remove it from anything we control.

Children's personal data

This deserves its own heading, because the majority of our students are between ten and seventeen years old and much of what we hold is about a child.

We treat the parent or legal guardian as the person entitled to give and withdraw consent, to see the records, and to ask for corrections or deletion. We act on a parent's instruction, not a student's, for anything concerning the records themselves.

We do not profile children for advertising, we do not target advertising at them, and we do not use their information for anything beyond teaching them and administering their course.

Why we use it

  • To answer your enquiry. Someone from the counselling team calls or writes back.
  • To run the course — allocate a batch, mark attendance, set and return tests, share material, arrange online class access.
  • To talk to you about your child: progress, attendance, test results, what needs work.
  • To collect and record fees, and to issue receipts.
  • To tell you about batches and courses relevant to the student. You can ask us to stop at any time and we will.
  • To keep the records the law requires us to keep, and to respond if a legal or regulatory authority lawfully asks.

We will not use your information for a purpose unconnected to the above without asking you first.

We do not sell, rent, barter or share your information for anyone else's marketing. If another coaching institute, publisher or agent ever contacts you claiming to have got your number from us, they did not.

Cookies and tracking

This page sets no cookies of its own, runs no analytics, and carries no advertising or tracking pixels. We genuinely do not know who visits it or what they read.

Your browser does fetch typefaces from Google Fonts, and a video card loads nothing from YouTube until you click it. Those requests reveal your IP address to them, which is how the web works for any site that loads an outside resource.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to admission — up to three years, because families often return a season or two later for a younger child.
  • Student and parent records — while the student is with us, and for eight years afterwards, so we can confirm a past student's attendance or results if they ask us to.
  • Fee and payment records — eight years, as our tax and accounting obligations require.
  • Photographs and video — until you ask us to remove them, or until we no longer have a use for them.

You can ask us to delete sooner. We will, except where a law requires us to keep a particular record for a stated period — and if we must keep something, we will tell you what and why.

How we protect it

Our records are held in a password-protected system, access is limited to staff who need it for their work, and this page is served only over an encrypted connection.

We will not pretend this is a guarantee. No institute of our size can promise that a determined attack will never succeed. What we can promise is that we collect as little as we can manage, that we do not pass it around, and that if a breach ever affects your information we will tell you rather than hope you do not notice.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask us to:

  • Show you what we hold about you or your child, and who we have shared it with.
  • Correct or complete anything that is wrong or out of date.
  • Erase what we no longer need for a lawful purpose.
  • Withdraw a consent you previously gave — for photographs, or for batch and course messages.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf if you are unable to.

Write to us at the address below. We do not charge for this. We will acknowledge within seven working days and answer within thirty days, and if we cannot do what you asked we will tell you the reason rather than go quiet.
