PRIVACY POLICY

1. What this page covers

This policy explains what WEDA — The Winning Edge Defence Academy collects from people who use this landing page, why, and what we do with it.

2. What we collect

  • Demo registration: parent's name, student's name, mobile number, email address (optional), target class and preferred learning mode.
  • Payment: a Razorpay order reference, payment reference and payment status. Card numbers, UPI IDs, net-banking credentials and similar payment details are entered on Razorpay's own secure checkout and are never seen or stored by WEDA.
  • WhatsApp: if you use an admission or enquiry button, you open a WhatsApp chat with us. What you send in that chat is held in WhatsApp under their terms.

This page sets no advertising or tracking cookies of its own.

3. Why we collect it

  • To reserve your child's place in the demo batch and confirm your payment.
  • To contact you about the batch schedule and the admission process.
  • To meet our record-keeping and tax obligations.

4. Who else sees it

We share what is necessary with Razorpay, who process the payment, and with our own counselling and academic staff. We do not sell your information, and we do not pass it to third parties for their own marketing.

5. How long we keep it

Registration and payment records are kept for as long as needed for our accounting and legal obligations. If you want your details removed earlier, contact us and we will delete whatever we are not required to retain.

6. Children's information

The students this page concerns are minors. We ask for a student's name and target class only, and we expect a parent or guardian to register on the child's behalf. We do not ask children to give us information directly.

7. Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us using the details below and tell us the mobile number you registered with.
