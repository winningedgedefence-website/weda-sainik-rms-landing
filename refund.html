REFUND POLICY

Last updated 21 November 2026 · The Winning Edge Defence Academy, Dehradun

We would rather you knew this before you paid than discovered it afterwards. We do not refund fees. Where something goes wrong at our end, we issue a WEDA coupon instead. It is one short page.

No payment is taken on this page. Nothing here asks for card, UPI or net-banking details, and no amount is collected online. A fee is paid later, at our counter or into the academy's own account, after a counsellor has spoken to you.

1. Fees are not refunded

Once a fee is paid and a seat is confirmed, it is not returned — whether the student attends or not. This covers the demo registration fee and course fees alike.

In particular, we do not refund — in whole or in part — where:

  • The student withdraws, at any stage, for any reason.
  • The student stops attending, or attends irregularly.
  • The student does not attend the demo days that were booked.
  • The family relocates, or the student's school timetable changes.
  • The student is not selected in the examination they were preparing for.
  • The student is removed from the course for misconduct under clause 6 of our Terms & Conditions.
  • The student's eligibility fails on age, category, medical grounds or document verification — matters decided by the examining authority, not by us.

Unpaid instalments remain payable for the session enrolled.

2. Why we work this way

When a seat is allotted, we stop offering it to anyone else. Faculty are engaged for the session, the batch size is set, and material is printed against the names on the roll. A student who withdraws in week six does not free up a seat another family can still use, and the cost of holding it has already been spent.

This is also why the demo exists. Sit in the classes first, meet the faculty, ask us hard questions — and decide after that.

3. The coupon

Where something has gone wrong at our end, or where a family's circumstances genuinely stop a student continuing, we issue a WEDA coupon rather than return the money.

  • It is issued in writing to the parent or guardian who paid, and names the amount and the student.
  • It can be used against any WEDA course, batch or session — classroom or online — including for a sibling.
  • It can be put towards a larger fee; the balance is payable as usual.
  • It cannot be exchanged for cash, and it cannot be sold or transferred outside the family.

Two situations where we issue one without making you argue for it:

  • A batch we cancel before it begins. If we cancel a batch outright before its first class, you may take a coupon for the full amount or a place in another batch, whichever you prefer. We will offer you both. This does not apply to a change of timing, a change of faculty, a batch moved online, or a rescheduled class — those are covered in clause 5 of the Terms & Conditions, and in each case we still deliver the teaching hours you paid for.
  • A genuine medical or family reason that stops a student continuing. Come and talk to us before you write the fee off. We have made arrangements before.

4. Transfers

A coupon is not the only thing we can do. At our discretion, and on a written request from the parent, we may:

  • Move a student to a different batch of the same course — a later start, or a different timing.
  • Move a student between modes — classroom to online, or the reverse — with any difference in fee adjusted either way.
  • Hold a paid fee against the next session.

These are concessions, not entitlements, and we decide them case by case.

5. If we have charged you in error

If we have taken money you did not owe us — the wrong course, the wrong amount, or the same amount twice — that was never a fee, and we put it right. Tell us as soon as you spot it, with the receipt number or transaction reference.

6. How to raise a request

Write to us — email or a letter at the counter, not a verbal request, so there is a record on both sides.

  • Email — winningedgedefence@gmail.com, with “Refund” in the subject line.
  • Include the student's name, the course and batch, the receipt number or transaction reference, the amount, and what you are asking for.
  • We acknowledge within three working days and give you a decision within fifteen working days.
  • An approved coupon is issued within twenty-one working days of the decision.

7. If you disagree with our decision

Ask for it to be reviewed by the Director. Say so in writing and it will be looked at again by someone who did not take the first decision.

Nothing on this page takes away a right you have under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 or any other applicable law. Any dispute is subject to the jurisdiction of the courts at Dehradun, Uttarakhand, as set out in our Terms & Conditions.

8. Changes to this policy

We may revise this page. The version in force on the date you paid is the one that applies to that payment — a later change does not reach backwards.
