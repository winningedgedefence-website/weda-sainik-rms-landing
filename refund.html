DEMO REGISTRATION & REFUND

In one line: the ₹500 registration fee is fully refundable. Once your child has completed the 5+1 Day Risk-Free Demo Classes, if your child does not want to continue, contact our team within 72 hours and the ₹500 is returned to you.

1. What this fee is

A fee of ₹500 is collected when you register for the 5+1 Day Risk-Free Demo Classes.

2. When the fee is refunded

SituationOutcome
Your child completes the demo classes and does not want to continue₹500 refunded in full

3. The 72-hour window

Tell us within 72 hours of your child's last demo class. Message us on WhatsApp at +91 74176 56633 or call +91 74179 20356, with the mobile number used at registration and your payment reference.

A request made after that 72-hour window cannot be processed, because the seat has by then been held and the batch planned around it.

4. How the refund reaches you

Approved refunds are returned through Razorpay to the original payment method. The amount normally reaches the account within 5 to 7 working days of approval, depending on your bank or card issuer. WEDA does not refund to a different account, and does not refund in cash.

5. What is not refundable

  • A request made more than 72 hours after the last demo class.
  • Regular course fees, which are governed by the fee terms given at the time of admission.

6. Changes to this policy

A change to this policy applies only to registrations made after it is published here. The terms shown on the day you paid are the terms that apply to your payment.
