DEMO REGISTRATION & REFUND

Last updated: 10 November 2026

In one line: the ₹500 demo registration fee is refundable once your child has completed the 5+1 Day Demo Classes. If you choose to continue into the extended 14-day trial instead, the fee is not refunded — it is treated as payment towards those additional days.

1. What this fee is

A fee of ₹500 is collected at the time of registering for the 5+1 Day Demo Classes. It is a registration and seat-reservation fee. It is not the course fee for any regular batch, and paying it does not enrol a student in a regular batch.

2. When the fee is refunded

SituationOutcome
Your child completes the 5+1 Day Demo Classes₹500 refunded in full
You continue into the extended 14-day trialNot refunded — retained against the additional days
WEDA cancels or cannot run the demo batch₹500 refunded in full
Duplicate or failed payment charged to youRefunded in full

3. How to request a refund

Message us on WhatsApp at +91 74176 56633 or call +91 74179 20356 with the registration reference shown on your confirmation screen and the mobile number used at registration.

4. How the refund reaches you

Approved refunds are returned through Razorpay to the original payment method. The amount normally reaches the account within 5 to 7 working days of approval, depending on your bank or card issuer. WEDA does not refund to a different account or by cash.

5. What is not refundable

  • The fee once the extended 14-day trial has begun.
  • Regular course fees, which are governed by the fee terms given at the time of admission.

6. Changes to this policy

A change to this policy applies only to registrations made after it is published here. The terms shown on the day you paid are the terms that apply to your payment.
