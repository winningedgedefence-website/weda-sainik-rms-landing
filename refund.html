REFUND POLICY

Last updated 21 November 2026 · The Winning Edge Defence Academy, Dehradun

We would rather you knew this before you paid than discovered it afterwards. Our fees are non-refundable. Where a refund would once have been given, we issue a WEDA coupon instead. Please read the one page.

The short version

  • Fees are not refunded. Not the ₹500 demo registration fee, and not course fees once a seat is confirmed — whether the student attends or not.
  • You get a coupon, not your money back. Where we accept that something went wrong at our end, we issue a WEDA coupon for the amount, usable against any of our courses or batches.
  • Money that was never a fee is returned. A duplicate payment, a payment that failed but still left your account, or an amount we charged in error comes back to you.

That is the whole policy. The rest of this page explains it, because a rule you understand is one you are less likely to be upset by.

No payment is taken on this page. The demo registration fee is paid at our counter or into the academy's own account after a counsellor has spoken to you, and this policy applies to it from the moment it is paid.

Why we work this way

When a seat is allotted, we stop offering it to anyone else. Faculty are engaged for the session, the batch size is set, and material is printed against the names on the roll. A student who withdraws in week six does not free up a seat that another family can still use, and the cost of holding it has already been spent.

This is also why the demo exists. We would rather you spent ₹500 finding out whether we suit your child than a full course fee discovering it months later. Use the demo days properly — sit in the classes, meet the faculty, ask us hard questions.

1. The demo registration fee — ₹500

₹500 gives a student 5+1 days of attendance in a live batch, with the same faculty, classes and material as an enrolled student.

  • It is non-refundable in every case. Not if you decide against enrolling, not if the student attends only part of the demo, not if the student does not turn up at all.
  • It is not adjusted against course fees unless a counsellor has confirmed that to you in writing.
  • It carries no obligation to enrol. That is the point of it.

Please book the demo only when you know the student can attend it. Once booked, treat the ₹500 as spent.

2. Course fees

Course fees are non-refundable once a seat is confirmed. A seat is confirmed when we receive the fee.

In particular, we do not refund — in whole or in part — where:

  • The student withdraws, at any stage, for any reason.
  • The student stops attending, or attends irregularly.
  • The family relocates, or the student's school timetable changes.
  • The student is not selected in the examination they were preparing for.
  • The student is removed from the course for misconduct under clause 6 of our Terms & Conditions.
  • The student's eligibility fails on age, category, medical grounds or document verification — matters decided by the examining authority, not by us.

Unpaid instalments remain payable for the session enrolled.

3. The coupon, instead of a refund

Where something has gone wrong at our end, or where a family's circumstances genuinely stop a student continuing, we do not return the fee — we issue a WEDA coupon for the amount we accept.

  • The coupon is issued in writing to the parent or guardian who paid, and names the amount and the student.
  • It can be used against any WEDA course, batch or session — classroom or online — including for a sibling.
  • It can be put towards a larger fee; the balance is payable as usual.
  • It cannot be exchanged for cash, and it cannot be sold or transferred outside the family.

Two situations where we issue one without making you argue for it:

  • A batch we cancel before it begins. If we cancel a batch outright before its first class, you may take a coupon for the full course fee or a place in another batch, whichever you prefer. We will offer you both. This does not apply to a change of timing, a change of faculty, a batch moved online, or a rescheduled class — those are covered in clause 5 of the Terms & Conditions, and in each case we still deliver the teaching hours you paid for.
  • A genuine medical or family reason that stops a student continuing. Come and talk to us before you write the fee off. We have made arrangements before.

4. Money we do return

Some money was never a fee, and we do not keep it.

  • A duplicate payment. If you are charged twice for the same fee, the extra amount comes back in full.
  • A failed payment. If money left your account for a transaction that did not complete, send us the transaction reference and we will trace it.
  • An amount we charged in error. If we have billed you for the wrong course, the wrong duration or the wrong amount, we correct it and return the difference. Tell us as soon as you spot it.

These are returned to the same account the payment came from. We do not pay in cash or to a third party's account. Bank charges already deducted by the provider are not ours to return.

5. Transfers

A coupon is not the only thing we can do. At our discretion, and on a written request from the parent, we may:

  • Move a student to a different batch of the same course — a later start, or a different timing.
  • Move a student between modes — classroom to online, or the reverse — with any difference in fee adjusted either way.
  • Hold a paid fee against the next session.

These are concessions, not entitlements, and we decide them case by case.

6. How to raise a request

Write to us — email or a letter at the counter, not a verbal request, so there is a record on both sides.

  • Email — winningedgedefence@gmail.com, with “Refund” in the subject line.
  • Include the student's name, the course and batch, the receipt number or transaction reference, the amount, and what you are asking for.
  • We acknowledge within three working days and give you a decision within fifteen working days.
  • An approved coupon is issued, or an approved return of money paid, within twenty-one working days of the decision.

7. If you disagree with our decision

Ask for it to be reviewed by the Director. Say so in writing and it will be looked at again by someone who did not take the first decision.

Nothing on this page takes away a right you have under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 or any other applicable law. Any dispute is subject to the jurisdiction of the courts at Dehradun, Uttarakhand, as set out in our Terms & Conditions.

8. Changes to this policy

We may revise this page. The version in force on the date you paid is the one that applies to that payment — a later change does not reach backwards.
